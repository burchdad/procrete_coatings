Answers before the estimate.

Does a rubber pool deck get hot?

Every outdoor surface can warm up in direct sun, but rubber surfacing is selected for a cooler, more comfortable barefoot feel than many hard concrete finishes.

Is it slippery around a pool?

The system is designed with a textured, non-skid profile for wet areas like pool decks, splash pads, and patios.

Can this go over cracked or damaged concrete?

Many projects can be installed over existing concrete after proper inspection and preparation.

How long does installation take?

Timing depends on square footage, repairs, weather, and access. ProCoat confirms timing during the estimate.

How do we keep it clean?

The finished surface is designed for heavy use and can be pressure washed when needed.

Can we choose the color?

Yes. ProCoat can recommend blends that coordinate with the home, pool, patio, garage, or commercial environment.

How much does it cost?

Cost depends on size, surface condition, prep, finish choice, and project complexity.

Is there a warranty?

ProCoat offers a lifetime warranty against chipping, peeling, or fading. Ask for full warranty details with your quote.
