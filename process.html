The process

A premium surface should feel organized from estimate to final walkthrough.

ProCoat’s process is built to reduce uncertainty: understand the surface, design the finish, prepare the concrete, install cleanly, and hand back a space that is ready to enjoy.

Start with photos

Five steps

Clear enough to trust, detailed enough to matter.

Step 01

Consult

Share photos, measurements, access, drainage, cracks, and how the space is used.

Step 02

Design

Choose color direction, texture, borders, and project scope before install begins.

Step 03

Prepare

Concrete is evaluated and mechanically prepared for the selected system.

Step 04

Install

Rubber granules, resin, edges, and finish details are installed with care.

Step 05

Enjoy

The surface is ready for better pool days, easier cleanup, and a finished look.
